Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Overview

AREITU does not run its own server. Your data is never sent to a server operated by the developer, and the developer has no access to it. Your visit-log data (an SQLite database) is stored in the app's private area on your iPhone (the iOS sandbox). If you sign in with your Google account, a copy of this database is also stored in the appDataFolder of your own Google Drive. This folder is not shown in the normal Google Drive UI and is not accessible to anything other than AREITU's OAuth client.

AREITU does not add its own encryption to the database. The on-device file is covered by iOS data protection; the copy on Drive is covered by Google's encryption at rest.

Data handled

  • Capture time and location (latitude/longitude) read from photos in your iPhone photo library, to which you have granted access. The photos themselves are not sent off the device and are not copied into the app.
  • Times, coordinates and place names read from a Google Timeline (Takeout) file that you choose to import in the app. The file itself is only read on your device and is not sent anywhere.
  • Visits derived from them (times, coordinates, inferred place names).
  • Only if you allow calendar access: titles and time ranges of events in your iPhone calendar (EventKit). AREITU does not add or change events.

Google OAuth scopes requested

Requests only drive.appdata. Used only to upload and download the visit-log database (areitu-ios.db) to and from the appDataFolder of your own Google Drive. The same folder also holds a backup copy created when a backup conflict would otherwise overwrite data (areitu-ios-conflict-<timestamp>.db). AREITU does not use Google Calendar: it reads calendar events with iPhone's EventKit, so it does not request any Google Calendar permission.

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata

When data leaves your device

The following is everything AREITU sends over the network. Each destination also receives your IP address as part of the connection.

  • Place lookup (reverse geocoding): to find the place name of a visit, AREITU sends the visit's latitude and longitude to Apple MapKit (point-of-interest search and address lookup). Apple handles this data under Apple's privacy policy. Coordinates already stored in the in-app dictionary are not sent.
  • Place-name inference by an on-device LLM (off by default): runs only if you turn it on, using Apple's on-device Foundation Models. Prompts are not sent off the device. On unsupported devices the feature is unavailable and the app works without it.
  • Google sign-in and backup: if you sign in, AREITU connects to accounts.google.com, oauth2.googleapis.com (authorization, tokens, revocation) and www.googleapis.com (Drive). While you are signed in, a backup runs automatically once every 6 hours during sync, and you can also run one with “今すぐバックアップ” (Back up now) in settings. Each backup saves areitu-ios.db to Drive.

AREITU sends nothing else. It sends no data for advertising or analytics.

Google user data (Limited Use)

AREITU's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • Drive data is used only to back up and restore your database.
  • Google user data is not transferred to the developer or to third parties.
  • Google user data is not used for advertising.
  • The developer does not read Google user data (the developer has no access to it).

Where data is stored

  • Visit database: the app-private container in the sandbox
  • Backup state: in the same container
  • Google refresh token: iOS Keychain only (this device only; it is not moved to other devices or backups)
  • Settings: the app's settings store (AREITU does not handle API keys)
  • Drive copy: areitu-ios.db in the appDataFolder of your Google account

How to delete your data

  1. Tap “この端末のデータをすべて削除” (Delete all data on this device) in the app's settings to delete the visit-log database, sync and backup state, the on-device LLM settings and your Google sign-in on this device, and sign out of Google (the token is removed from the Keychain and a revocation request is sent to Google). Your backup on Google Drive is not deleted.
  2. Signing out of Google in settings removes the token from the Keychain and asks Google to revoke it. The backup on Drive remains.
  3. To delete the backup on Drive, open Google Drive Settings → Manage apps and delete AREITU's hidden app data. You can remove AREITU's access on your Google Account's Third-party access page (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
  4. Deleting AREITU from your iPhone also deletes the on-device database, sync and backup state, and the on-device LLM settings. On iOS, however, the Google token in the Keychain may remain after the app is deleted, so sign out of Google before deleting the app.
